Grade Level: Beginner
Subject: Drums
Topic: Basic Drum Beats
Duration: 40 minutes

Lesson Objectives:
- Learn basic stick control
- Understand simple drum patterns
- Play a basic rock beat

Prior Knowledge:
- None needed. First-time drummers welcome!

Introduction:
- Briefly explain what a drum set is
- Demonstrate hitting snare, bass, and hi-hat

Body of the Lesson:
Concept 1: Basic Stick Control
- Practice single strokes (R L R L)

Concept 2: Basic Rock Beat
- Hi-hat on every beat
- Snare on 2 and 4
- Bass on 1 and 3

Activity:
- Play along with a simple song ("Seven Nation Army")

Assessment:
- Can they keep a steady beat for 1 minute?

Extension:
- Try adding simple fills (small rolls between beats)

Conclusion:
- Review what we learned and celebrate the progress!
